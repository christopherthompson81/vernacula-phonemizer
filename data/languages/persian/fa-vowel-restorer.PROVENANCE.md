# `fa-vowel-restorer.{enc,dec}.onnx` — provenance

**What it is.** A character-level seq2seq model (BiLSTM encoder, attention decoder; two int8 ONNX graphs) that
maps a Persian abjad word to IPA. It is the OOV tier for short-vowel restoration.

**Training data.**
- wikipron `fas_arab` broad: abjad→IPA pairs, human transcriptions from Wiktionary. CC BY-SA.
- A Tajik-derived cross-script silver set, `tools/persian/fa-tg-silver.tsv`. The Tajik text comes from
  wikipron and tgwiki (CC BY-SA); the IPA is generated by this repo.

**Licence.** The weights are declared CC-BY-SA-inheriting (`LICENSES/PROVENANCE.md` §3).

**Regenerate.** `tools/persian/export_s2s_onnx.py` (torch + CUDA).

Build notes and measurements: `docs/investigations/fa/fa-vowel-restorer_build_notes_investigation.md`.
