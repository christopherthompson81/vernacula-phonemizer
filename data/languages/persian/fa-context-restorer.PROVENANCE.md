# `fa-context-restorer.{enc,dec}.onnx` — provenance

**What it is.** A character-level, sentence-level seq2seq model (BiLSTM encoder, attention decoder; two int8
ONNX graphs) that maps a whole Persian abjad hemistich to IPA. It serves the optional classical-Persian path.

**Training data.** The aligned Shahnameh parallel corpus in `tools/persian/parallel/`, 39k hemistichs:
- the Persian text of Ferdowsi's *Shahnameh*, public domain (d. 1020). The digital edition was obtained via
  Ganjoor (ganjoor.net), whose digitization terms are unstated. It is treated as a faithful transcription of
  a public-domain work, which creates no new copyright under the facts-not-expression analysis;
- a Tajik edition, CC BY-SA;
- Tajik-derived silver IPA generated by this repo.

**Licence.** The weights are declared CC-BY-SA-inheriting because of the Tajik component
(`LICENSES/PROVENANCE.md` §1 and §3).

**Regenerate.** `tools/persian/export_context_onnx.py` (GPU).

Build notes and measurements: `docs/investigations/fa/fa-context-restorer_build_notes_investigation.md`.
